Also @sarah_design — the color contrast on the CTA button is failing WCAG AA. The text-to-bg ratio on the teal button looks like ~2.8:1, you need 4.5:1 minimum for normal text.
WCAG 2.1 — Contrast Minimum (Level AA)
Text and images of text have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1. Large text at least 3:1.
