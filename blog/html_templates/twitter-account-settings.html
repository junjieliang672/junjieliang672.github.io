About connected apps

These apps have been granted access to your X account using OAuth. Review them periodically and remove any you no longer use or don't recognize.

Access levels

Read — can view your posts and profile.

Read + Write — can post on your behalf.

Account Management — can modify account settings, including suspension controls. Grant only to fully trusted apps.

🔒
Apps with Account Management permission can deactivate or lock your account. Review these apps carefully.
