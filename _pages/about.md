<p class="lead-in">I work on AI security. At <a href="https://www.bytedance.com/en/">ByteDance</a>, I build automated red-teaming systems for large language models and AI agents.</p>

Before that, I spent three years at [Bloomberg AI](https://www.bloomberg.com/company/values/tech-at-bloomberg/artificial-intelligence-ai/) working on information retrieval. I earned my Ph.D. at [Penn State](https://ist.psu.edu/), advised by [Dr. Vasant G. Honavar](http://faculty.ist.psu.edu/vhonavar/) in the [AI Lab](https://ailab.ist.psu.edu/index.html), where I worked on machine learning for longitudinal data. Before my Ph.D., I worked with [Dr. Jinlong Hu](https://largeapp.github.io/) at [South China University of Technology](http://www2.scut.edu.cn/cs_en/) during my master's degree.

<span class="font-weight-bold">Research interests:</span> AI security (LLMs and agents), information retrieval, natural language understanding, longitudinal data analysis.

<p class="aside">Outside work, I snowboard, play badminton, lift weights, and enjoy math and RTS games; my all-time favorites are <a href="https://www.ageofempires.com/games/aoeiide/">AOE2</a> and <a href="https://www.klei.com/games/dont-starve-together">Don't Starve Together</a>. I also build small open-source tools that I use every day, such as <a href="https://github.com/junjieliang672/marmota">Marmota</a>, a terminal file manager that works beside coding agents (<a href="https://junjieliang672.github.io/marmota/tutorial.html">Take the tour</a>), and <a href="https://github.com/junjieliang672/MarmotIM">MarmotIM</a>, a Wubi–Pinyin input method for macOS.</p>
